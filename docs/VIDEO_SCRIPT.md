# 90 second walkthrough: script and shot list

Record with any screen recorder (Loom, QuickTime, OBS) at 1440 wide using the live demo. Speak in the plain language below. Aim for a calm pace; about 200 words fit in 90 seconds. After uploading, paste the video link into the README line "Watch the 90 second walkthrough".

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Dashboard top, headline visible | "Companies spend heavily on online ads, and the ad platforms grade their own homework. This tool checks whether the money is really earning its keep." |
| 0:10 to 0:25 | Point to the sentence under the headline, then the four confidence cards | "Here the platforms claim nearly four hundred thousand dollars more than controlled tests support. And these cards show how sure we are: six of eight campaigns have strong enough evidence to act on." |
| 0:25 to 0:35 | Click the CFO / Finance view | "Each leader gets their own view. The finance chief sees which campaigns lost money and by how much." |
| 0:35 to 0:45 | Scroll to Audience tiers, point at the red and green bar | "Platforms often credit themselves for people who were going to buy anyway. Here, about ninety thousand dollars of spend sits in tiers where most of the credited sales would have happened without the ads." |
| 0:45 to 0:55 | Scroll to the advisory council | "Four advisors read the same results from different angles, so you see where opinions agree and where judgement is needed. They suggest; they never instruct." |
| 0:55 to 1:03 | Scroll to a chart, click Table | "Every chart tells you its finding and can switch to a table you can copy into a spreadsheet." |
| 1:03 to 1:20 | Open the Sign-off desk, open a decision, tick and sign | "Nothing changes without a person. Each decision is signed with a note and a role, and recorded so that any later edit would show." |
| 1:20 to 1:30 | Scroll to the log, point at Log intact | "That is the idea: clear answers, honest uncertainty, and people stay in charge. The data is synthetic and the platform names are only labels." |

## Tips
* Do one practice run so the page is already loaded when you start.
* Keep the mouse slow. Pause one second after each click.
* Do not read numbers you cannot see on screen.
